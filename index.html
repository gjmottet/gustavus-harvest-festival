🏆 OFFICIAL JUDGING & STICKER THRESHOLDS

Sticker Awards by Points (applies to all classes except Race, Largest Cabbage, Largest Potato, Zucchini 500):
🟦 Blue Sticker — 100pt: 90-100 | 50pt: 45-50 | 25pt: 22-25
🟥 Red Sticker — 100pt: 80-89 | 50pt: 40-44 | 25pt: 20-21
⬜ White Sticker — 100pt: 70-79 | 50pt: 35-39 | 25pt: 16-19
🟢 Green Sticker — 100pt: ≤69 | 50pt: ≤34 | 25pt: ≤15

🏁 Race & Weight Classes (no stickers): Chicken & Rabbit Race: 1st, 2nd, 3rd place ribbons. Largest Cabbage, Largest Potato, Zucchini 500: 1st place ribbon only.

🏅 ROSETTE AWARDS (all rosettes):
🐇 Grand Champion Rabbit Rosette — highest combined total points from Rabbit (Class 1) + Cage Decor (Class 9, if entered with that rabbit) + Tanned Rabbit Skin (Class 10).
🐇 Reserve Champion Rabbit Rosette — second highest combined total.
🐔 Grand Champion Chicken Rosette — Chicken (Class 3) + Eggs (Class 7) + Cage Decor (Class 9).
🐔 Reserve Champion Chicken Rosette — second highest.
🌟 Best in Show Rosette — highest overall cumulative points across ALL entries (any department).

Note: Cage Decor and Showmanship can be entered once per animal. Cage Decor offers +5 bonus points for incorporating Harvest Festival theme.

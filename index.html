🏆 OFFICIAL JUDGING & STICKER THRESHOLDS

Sticker Awards by Points (applies to all classes except Race, Largest Cabbage, Largest Potato, Zucchini 500):
🟦 Blue Sticker — 100pt: 90–100 | 50pt: 45–50 | 25pt: 22–25
🟥 Red Sticker — 100pt: 80–89 | 50pt: 40–44 | 25pt: 20–21
⬜ White Sticker — 100pt: 70–79 | 50pt: 35–39 | 25pt: 16–19
🟢 Green Sticker — 100pt: ≤69 | 50pt: ≤34 | 25pt: ≤15

🏁 Race & Weight Classes scoring: Chicken & Rabbit Race / Zucchini 500 — 1st place 25pts, 2nd place 20pts, 3rd place and below 15pts participation. Largest Cabbage / Largest Potato — heaviest entry 25pts, all others 15pts participation.

🏅 ROSETTE AWARDS (all rosettes):
🐇 Grand Champion Rabbit Rosette — highest combined total points from Rabbit (Class 1) + Cage Decor (Class 9, if entered with that rabbit) + Tanned Rabbit Skin (Class 10).
🐇 Reserve Champion Rabbit Rosette — second highest combined total points from Rabbit + Cage Decor + Rabbit Skin.
🐔 Grand Champion Chicken Rosette — highest combined total points from Chicken (Class 3) + Eggs (Class 7) + Cage Decor (Class 9, if paired with that chicken).
🐔 Reserve Champion Chicken Rosette — second highest combined total points from Chicken + Eggs + Cage Decor.
🌟 Best in Show Rosette — awarded to the exhibitor with the highest overall cumulative points across ALL entries (any department).

Note: Cage Decor and Showmanship can be entered once per animal. Cage Decor offers +5 bonus points for incorporating the Harvest Festival theme. Showmanship points add to exhibitor's total for Best in Show but not to species champion combos.

⭐ Scoring Note: For all judging criteria, a higher score indicates better quality, health, creativity, or presentation. Judges assign points accordingly.
